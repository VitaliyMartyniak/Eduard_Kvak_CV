Intelas

Intelas is the leading provider of financial data standardization, serving real estate asset managers and accountants. The company specializes in collecting and standardizing property-level data, ensuring accuracy and consistency across financial reporting and analysis. This project focuses on developing robust data management solutions to streamline financial operations and enhance decision-making processes for industry professionals.
